Our Journey & Legacy

Next Level Gaming launched in 2023 with a bold vision: to redefine the South African gaming landscape. Born in Rosebank, Johannesburg, we set out to build an inclusive esports sanctuary with zero compromises on performance.

From our custom-designed neon layout to our advanced hardware testing protocols, we make sure that South African creators, competitors, and gamers have the exact same platform tools as pro-circuit global squads.
